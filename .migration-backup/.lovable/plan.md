

## Konstantine — Personal Portfolio Website

### Design System
- **Dark theme** with near-black backgrounds (#0A0A0A), white text, subtle gray borders
- **Glassmorphism navigation bar** — frosted glass effect with backdrop blur, semi-transparent background, rounded pill shape (inspired by iOS liquid glass)
- **Accent color**: subtle warm white/cream for highlights
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif, large bold headings, elegant spacing
- **Cards**: Dark glass-effect cards with subtle borders and hover animations

### Pages (7 total)

#### 1. Homepage
- **Hero section**: Large name "Konstantine" with animated subtitle cycling through skills (Filmmaker · Developer · Social Media Strategist · Events Manager · Model/Actor)
- **Skills/strengths radar or grid**: Visual representation of skill areas with relative strength — designed to be easily updated
- **Industry experience badges**: E-commerce, Technology/Startups, Real Estate, EV Industry
- **Education & certifications**: Sobey School of Business, Harvard CS Summer Program, Full-time Filmmaker Certificate, HubSpot Social Media & SEO
- **Featured projects carousel** pulling highlights from each service area
- **CTA**: "Book a Call" button

#### 2. Modeling & Acting Portfolio
- Photo/video grid gallery with filtering (Modeling vs Acting)
- Project cards with details
- CTA: "Book Me" / "View Reel"

#### 3. Social Media & Influencer Management
- Services offered (account management, influencer partnerships, content strategy)
- Case study cards with metrics placeholders
- CTA: "Hire Me to Coach You & Your Team"

#### 4. Events — Organizing & Management
- Event types and services
- Past events showcase grid
- CTA: "Book a Call" to discuss events

#### 5. Web & App Development
- Services and tech capabilities
- Project showcase cards
- CTA: "Hire Me to Coach You & Your Team"

#### 6. Business Development
- Networking, outreach, industry partnerships
- Industries served with icons
- CTA: "Let's Connect"

#### 7. Contact Page
- Contact form (name, email, service interest dropdown, message)
- Social media links
- Calendar/booking link integration placeholder

### Navigation
- **Sticky glassmorphism nav bar** — frosted glass pill with logo "Konstantine", nav links (About, Portfolio dropdown, Services dropdown, Contact), and "Book a Call" CTA button
- Mobile: hamburger menu with slide-in glass panel
- Smooth scroll animations and page transitions

### Shared Components
- Footer with social links, quick nav, and copyright
- Animated section transitions on scroll (fade-in/slide-up)
- Consistent "Book a Call" floating or sticky CTA
